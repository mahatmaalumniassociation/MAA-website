OUR FOUNDER AND MENTOR

Every Mahatmite walked through a door she opened.

In 1983 she began Mahatma with 32 children and 6 teachers. Four decades on, the school she built is home to more than 11,000 students, and to every one of us who still carries its name.

Thank you, Ma'am.
